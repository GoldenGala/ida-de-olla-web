Delete your account and data

Ida de olla doesn't use email or passwords: your "account" is the cloud space shared by your linked devices. You can delete it from the app or by asking us by email.

From the app (quickest)

  1. Open Ida de olla and go to Profile.
  2. If you want to keep a copy, tap Export my recipes.
  3. Tap Delete my data from the cloud and confirm.

Your recipes, photos, weekly plan, lists, your membership of shared cookbooks, your name in them, your Google email if you connected it and your account are deleted from the cloud, also for your other linked devices. What is on your device stays there until you uninstall the app.

If you no longer have the app

Write to idadeolla@proton.me with the subject "Delete my Ida de olla account" and include one of these so we can find your account:

  • your recovery code, or
  • the Google email you connected to the app.

Without one of these there is no way to know which account is yours, because the app doesn't ask for an email or a name. We will reply within 30 days at most.

What is deleted and when

  • Recipes, photos, plan, lists, name in cookbooks and Google email: removed at the next sync and completely within 30 days at most.
  • Your recipes stop appearing in the shared cookbooks you were in.
  • The server's technical security logs are deleted automatically after a limited time.
