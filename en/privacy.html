Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

In short: Ida de olla doesn't ask for your email or your name to use it, has no ads and doesn't track you. Your recipes are stored on your device and, if you use syncing, on a server in the European Union that only your devices and the people you share a cookbook with can access. You can take your data with you and delete it whenever you want.

Who is responsible

The data controller is GoldenGala, developer of Ida de olla. Contact for any privacy matter: idadeolla@proton.me.

What data is stored and why

DataWhat forWhere
Your recipes, photos, weekly plan and shopping list For the app to work and, if you use it, to sync them between your devices On your device and, for syncing, on Supabase (European Union)
An anonymous identifier for each device and its type ("Phone", "PC") To link your devices without accounts or passwords Supabase (European Union)
The name you write for shared cookbooks (for example, "Ana") So the people in your cookbook know who is who. Only if you use cookbooks Supabase (European Union)
Your Google email, if you choose to connect Google To recover your recipes on a new device. It is optional Supabase (European Union)

Your data is not used for advertising, profiling or statistics, and it is not sold or given to anyone.

Legal basis

The data is processed because it is necessary to provide the service you request when you use the app (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Connecting Google and creating shared cookbooks are optional and based on your decision to use them.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase: hosts the syncing (database and photos) in a data centre in the European Union, as data processor. It keeps technical logs (for example, the IP address of connections) for a limited time for security.
  • Google: only if you connect your Google account (sign-in) or if you donate through Google Play. Payments are handled by Google Play; Ida de olla never sees your card details.
  • Websites you import recipes from: when you import a recipe from a website, your device downloads that page directly, as a browser would.
  • System dictation: if you import a recipe by voice, dictation is done by your device's speech recognition service (Android or Windows), under its own policy.

Text recognition in photos and documents happens on your own device: images are not sent to any server.

Shared cookbooks

If you share a recipe in a cookbook, the people in that cookbook will see it. If you leave the cookbook or delete your account, your recipes stop appearing in it. Imported recipes are for your private use: the app keeps the source link and does not publish them.

How long data is kept

  • While you use the app and don't delete your data.
  • What you delete is completely removed from the server after 30 days.
  • Codes to link devices expire after 10 minutes, and cookbook invitations after 7 days.
  • If you delete your data from the cloud, your recipes, photos, plan, lists and account are deleted.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete and take away (portability) your data, object to its processing or ask for it to be restricted:

  • Take it away: in the app, Profile → Export my recipes (a .zip file).
  • Delete it: in the app, Profile → Delete my data from the cloud. More details in Delete your account and data.
  • Any other request: write to idadeolla@proton.me.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or to the data protection authority of your country.

App permissions

The camera, microphone and files are only requested when you use them (to take a photo of a recipe, dictate it or open a document). Notifications and alarms are used so that cooking mode timers ring.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 14.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be published here with its date.
