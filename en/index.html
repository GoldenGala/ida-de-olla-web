Ida de olla Español

Your recipes, at hand

Ida de olla is a recipe app for Android and Windows: save your recipes, cook step by step with timers, plan the week and generate the shopping list. You can share cookbooks with your family or friends. Available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, German and Catalan.

Free and complete. No ads, no tracking and no selling of data. Donations are voluntary and don't unlock anything.

Works offline. Your recipes live on your device; the cloud is only used to sync your devices and your shared cookbooks.
